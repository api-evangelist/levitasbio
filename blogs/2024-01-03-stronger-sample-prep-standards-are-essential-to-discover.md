As a scientist, standardization and appropriately controlling your experiments are vitally important to ensure the questions addressed within those experiments can be answered. We know that too many variables can cause inconclusive results, preventing sample-to-sample comparability. Cell therapy researchers are experiencing variability from donors, patients, tumors, etc.
